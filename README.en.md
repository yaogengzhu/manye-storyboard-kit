# Manye Studio — Novel-to-Comic Storyboarding Guide

[简体中文](README.md)

Manye Studio (漫页 Studio) is an online AI comic creation tool for Chinese-language storytellers. Turn an original or licensed story into characters and shots, review the storyboard, generate comic images, and inspect each page before export.

This repository is maintained by Manye Studio and contains a product introduction, practical storyboarding guidance, and an original example. Visit the [Manye Studio website](https://manye.cc/) to use the online creation tool.

## Workflow

```text
Prepare a story → Define characters → Plan shots → Review the storyboard
→ Generate images → Inspect each page → Export all downloadable images as one ZIP
```

Start with a short scene that has a clear beginning and ending. Keep fixed character features separate from changing expressions, poses, and held objects. Give each shot one main action that can be depicted in a still image.

Review the text before generating images. Afterwards, check character identity, dialogue attribution, scene continuity, and reading order. Use the available editing or page regeneration controls to correct problems, and review the credit estimate before generating again.

## Original example: a rainy station

Lin Yao returns to a rainy station and discovers that her notebook is missing from a bench. A young man holding an umbrella offers it back. She takes the notebook, thanks him, and notices an orange cat peeking out from beneath the bench.

For this example, Lin Yao has short black hair, a white shirt, and a yellow backpack. The young man has short brown hair, a dark gray jacket, and a dark blue umbrella. The notebook is brown.

| Shot | Framing | Main action | Continuity check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Wide shot | Lin Yao runs up to the bench | The bench is empty; keep the rain and yellow backpack visible |
| 2 | Medium close-up | She looks down at the empty seat | Preserve the bench direction and backpack |
| 3 | Two-person medium shot | The young man offers the notebook | He still holds the notebook |
| 4 | Close-up of hands | Lin Yao takes it as he lets go | Show the handover of the same brown notebook |
| 5 | Close-up | She holds it to her chest and looks up | Lin Yao now owns the notebook; her appearance stays consistent |
| 6 | Low-angle medium shot | The cat peeks out; both characters look down | Keep the same station and bench |

Six shots do not necessarily mean six comic pages. A page may contain several panels; choose the layout according to the story and creation settings.

Use these fields in any text editor for manual planning. This template does not generate images or provide an automatic workspace import format.

## Review checklist

- Each shot maps to the original story; the author has approved added dialogue.
- Character identity remains recognizable, and appearance changes have a story reason.
- Object handovers and changes in position are understandable between shots.
- Dialogue belongs to the correct speaker and is readable at phone size.
- The final export has the intended order, current page versions, and no missing or duplicate pages.
